About Us

Into the green we go — to sculpt the world into what we imagine.

ABOUT AMBIUS LANDSCAPE

We're building sustainable ways to create beauty a whole new world creative ways to harness nature

We've been shaping India's landscape since 2014 under the aegis of renowned landscape Designer Mr. Vinod Mor — a distinguished fellow of the Indian Society of Landscape Designer and Developer.

Ambius Landscape, of our eponymous firm, has had a storied career in which he's dedicated himself to transforming the world he sees. He has learnt and honed his craft for decades, and has worked with two of India's premier landscape consultancy firms before embarking on his own independent journey. At Ambius Landscape, he is bringing intention, sustainability and an unmatched vision to the science and art of landscape design and developers.

GUIDING PRINCIPLES

Our Values

01

Listen, Explore, Learn

We're guided by the rhythm of nature and the ever-evolving landscape of design. We listen to everything, explore everything that catches our eye, and learn from every medium, partner, project, and collaborator. This allows us to maximise today's possibilities and to anticipate the future.

02

Ideas Come Through Dialogue

The hallmark of genius is collaboration, and so we bring diverse voices into every project to spark the next big idea. We talk, discuss, debate, even argue, and that's what provokes the freshest ideas that speak to the needs and aspirations of all.

03

Art and Evidence Must Coexist

We blend an artist's palette with a scientist's precision to find a balance between aesthetics and practicality. Everything that is beautiful serves a purpose. Everything that has function is also a piece of a larger, beautiful canvas.

We are committed to provide top class innovative solutions and excellent service delivery experience to our clients.

We believe in innovation & improvisation. We enhance our skills & update our technologies to give site-specific solutions.

OUR PURPOSE

Mission

Ambius Landscape is committed to the creation and delivery of eco-friendly products that blend harmoniously with nature. Ambius Landscape aims to make these sustainable green technologies a safe, easy and reliable way for the benefit of future generations & environment.

Leading the Horticulture & Landscape Industry by

  • Research, development and innovation of green methods and systems, required for sustainable landscape garden and terrace gardening.
  • Developing sustainable, cost-efficient, light-weight, stable growth-media.
  • Developing, producing and providing sustainable, environment-friendly products/services with warranties, required for creating biophilic-spaces and energy-efficient green-buildings to combat climate change.
